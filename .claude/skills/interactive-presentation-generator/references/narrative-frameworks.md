# Narrative Frameworks for Presentations

## Framework 1: Hero's Journey (Innovation & Change)

**Best for:** Transformation stories, product launches, change management, internal initiatives

**Structure:**
1. **Ordinary World** (Where we are now)
2. **Call to Adventure** (Something needs to change)
3. **Refusal of the Call** (Obstacles, fear)
4. **Meeting the Mentor** (Your solution appears)
5. **Crossing the Threshold** (Commitment to change)
6. **Tests & Trials** (Implementation challenges)
7. **Approach to Inmost Cave** (Key inflection point)
8. **Ordeal** (The climax/biggest challenge)
9. **Reward** (Benefits achieved)
10. **The Road Back** (Sustainability, long-term vision)
11. **Resurrection** (New normal, transformation complete)
12. **Return with the Elixir** (Legacy, impact)

**Slide Mapping (for 12-slide deck):**
- Slide 1: Ordinary World + Call to Adventure (hook)
- Slide 2: Refusal (the problem)
- Slide 3: Mentor appears (your solution intro)
- Slide 4: Benefits preview (reward teaser)
- Slide 5-6: Tests & Trials (how you'll implement)
- Slide 7: The Ordeal (biggest challenge + how you overcome)
- Slide 8-9: Reward (benefits, metrics, proof)
- Slide 10: Resurrection (new normal, sustainable)
- Slide 11: Elixir (legacy, broader impact)
- Slide 12: CTA (join the journey)

**Tone:** Inspirational, emotional, transformative  
**Use when:** You're asking people to embrace change, adopt something new, think differently

## Framework 2: Problem-Solution (Sales & Business)

**Best for:** Pitches, business cases, process improvement, vendor selection

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** (Grab attention with a provocative question or stat)
2. **Problem Statement** (What's broken?)
3. **Problem Impact** (Why should they care? What does it cost?)
4. **Root Cause** (Why is this happening?)
5. **Your Solution** (What you're proposing)
6. **How It Works** (Implementation overview)
7. **Proof Points** (Case studies, metrics, results)
8. **Comparison** (Why yours vs. alternatives)
9. **Benefits** (What improves? Quantified.)
10. **Timeline** (When? How fast?)
11. **Investment** (Cost, ROI)
12. **Call to Action** (Next step, decision, timeline)

**Slide Mapping (for 12-slide deck):**
- Slide 1: Hook (stat, question, provocative statement)
- Slides 2-3: Problem + Impact
- Slide 4: Root Cause
- Slides 5-6: Your Solution + How It Works
- Slides 7-8: Proof Points (2 strong examples)
- Slide 9: Benefits (quantified)
- Slide 10: Timeline + Investment
- Slide 11: ROI/Payback
- Slide 12: CTA (sign-up, trial, meeting, vote)

**Tone:** Logical, urgent, data-driven  
**Use when:** Asking for a business decision, funding, or adoption

## Framework 3: Case Study (Credibility & Proof)

**Best for:** Government reports, consultancy presentations, research findings, policy briefings

**Structure:**
1. **Situation** (Background, context, "the before")
2. **Challenge** (What problem did the client/organization face?)
3. **Your Approach** (What did you do? Methodology?)
4. **Timeline** (How long did it take?)
5. **Results** (Quantified outcomes)
6. **Results Breakdown** (Segment the wins: financial, operational, strategic)
7. **Lessons Learned** (What worked? What was unexpected?)
8. **Scalability** (Can this be repeated? How?)
9. **Client Quote/Testimonial** (Voice of success)
10. **Comparable Cases** (Similar wins elsewhere)
11. **Next Steps for Audience** (How to apply this)
12. **CTA** (Commit to same approach)

**Slide Mapping (for 12-slide deck):**
- Slide 1: Situation + Challenge (context + hook)
- Slide 2: Your Approach (visual overview)
- Slide 3: Timeline
- Slides 4-5: Results (headline + detailed breakdown)
- Slide 6: Lessons Learned
- Slide 7: Scalability (how to replicate)
- Slide 8: Testimonial (client quote, photo, credibility)
- Slide 9: Comparable Cases (social proof)
- Slide 10: How to Apply (audience's next steps)
- Slide 11: ROI/Benefits for them
- Slide 12: CTA (implement, adopt, commit)

**Tone:** Authoritative, evidence-based, practical  
**Use when:** Proving something works (government adoption, regulatory compliance, policy shift)

## Framework 4: Data Story (Analytics & Research)

**Best for:** Research presentations, analytics deep-dives, policy insights, trend reports

**Structure:**
1. **The Question** (What did we want to know?)
2. **Why It Matters** (Why should the audience care?)
3. **Hypothesis** (What did we expect to find?)
4. **Data & Methodology** (How did we measure?)
5. **Key Finding #1** (First insight, visualized)
6. **Key Finding #2** (Second insight, visualized)
7. **Key Finding #3** (Third insight, visualized)
8. **Unexpected Result** (Surprise, nuance)
9. **Interpretation** (What does it mean?)
10. **Conclusion** (So what? The big picture)
11. **Limitations** (What we couldn't measure, caveats)
12. **Next Steps** (Further research, application, decision needed)

**Slide Mapping (for 12-slide deck):**
- Slide 1: The Question + Why It Matters (hook with context)
- Slide 2: Hypothesis + Methodology
- Slides 3-5: Key Findings (one chart per slide, heavily visual)
- Slide 6: Unexpected Result (the plot twist)
- Slide 7: Interpretation (what it means)
- Slide 8: Conclusion (big picture takeaway)
- Slide 9: Limitations (honesty builds trust)
- Slide 10: Application (how does this inform decisions?)
- Slide 11: Further Research (what's next?)
- Slide 12: CTA (vote, allocate budget, launch initiative)

**Tone:** Curious, evidence-driven, honest, analytical  
**Use when:** Presenting research, asking for data-driven decisions

## Framework 5: Chronological (Process, History, Roadmap)

**Best for:** Training, onboarding, process overviews, project timelines, historical context

**Structure:**
1. **The Past** (How did we get here? History/context)
2. **Key Event #1** (First major decision/milestone)
3. **Key Event #2** (Second milestone)
4. **Key Event #3** (Third milestone, building momentum)
5. **Inflection Point** (Major change/shift)
6. **The Present** (Where we are now)
7. **What Works Now** (Current strengths)
8. **What Needs Improvement** (Current gaps)
9. **The Future** (Vision, next phase)
10. **Transition Path** (How we get there)
11. **Timeline** (Milestones, key dates)
12. **CTA** (What we need from you)

**Slide Mapping (for 12-slide deck):**
- Slide 1: The Past + Context (historical anchor)
- Slides 2-4: Key Milestones (3 major events, each on one slide)
- Slide 5: Inflection Point (the turning point)
- Slide 6: The Present (current state, "now")
- Slide 7: Strengths (what's working)
- Slide 8: Gaps (what needs improvement)
- Slide 9: The Future Vision (where we're going)
- Slide 10: Transition Path (how we'll get there, roadmap visual)
- Slide 11: Key Dates & Milestones (timeline)
- Slide 12: CTA (participation, support, commitment)

**Tone:** Narrative, instructional, forward-looking  
**Use when:** Training people on a process, orienting them to history/context, or showing a strategic roadmap

## How to Choose Your Framework

Ask yourself these questions:

**Q: Are you primarily asking for a DECISION?**
→ Problem-Solution or Case Study

**Q: Are you primarily asking for BELIEF CHANGE or ADOPTION?**
→ Hero's Journey or Problem-Solution

**Q: Are you presenting RESEARCH or ANALYSIS?**
→ Data Story or Case Study

**Q: Are you ORIENTING people to context/process/history?**
→ Chronological or Case Study

**Q: Are you PROVING something works?**
→ Case Study or Data Story

**Q: Are you LAUNCHING something new?**
→ Hero's Journey or Data Story (if you have pilot data)

---

## Hybrid Frameworks

You don't have to stick to one framework. Common combinations:

**Problem-Solution + Case Study:** Start with the problem (Problem-Solution), then prove it works with 2-3 real examples (Case Study elements)

**Hero's Journey + Data Story:** Inspire people with transformation story, then back it up with data and metrics

**Case Study + Chronological:** Present a case study, but structure it around a historical timeline to show evolution

## Framework Selection Template

When the skill asks for your presentation details, you can indicate:

```
Topic: [what you're presenting]
Audience: [who, size, technical level]
Primary Goal: [decision / belief / learning / action]
Suggested Framework: [Hero's Journey / Problem-Solution / Case Study / Data Story / Chronological / Unsure - let skill choose]
```

If you're unsure, the skill will analyze your topic + goal and recommend the best fit.
